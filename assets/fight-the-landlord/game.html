三人斗地主 · Classic scored rules

54 cards: 17 each, plus 3 face-up bottom cards for the landlord. Bid once: Pass, 1, 2, or 3; a bid must exceed the previous bid. Three ends bidding immediately. If all pass, redeal.

地主 leads. Turns go You → Bot 2 (right) → Bot 1 (left). Follow with a higher play of the same type, length, and attachments—or pass. Two consecutive passes give the last player a new lead. A pass does not stop you playing on a later turn.

Ranks: 大王 > 小王 > 2 > A > K > Q > J > 10 … 3. Suits have equal strength.

Sequences stop at A: never 2 or jokers. Wings do not change strength; compare the triple sequence or four-of-a-kind. Bare airplanes, single wings, and pair wings are different patterns.

Wing rules for this version: single wings may form a pair (333444 + 77 and 5555 + 77 are legal). Wing ranks must differ from the core; pair wings must be distinct pairs. Both jokers, an entire bomb, or an adjacent extra triple cannot be airplane wings. Both jokers cannot accompany four-of-a-kind.

Either farmer finishing wins for both farmers. Round stake = winning bid × 2 for each bomb/rocket × 2 for spring, if any. Landlord wins/loses twice the stake; each farmer loses/wins one stake.

春天: landlord wins before either farmer plays. 反春天: farmers win after the landlord played exactly once. This classic version goes directly from bidding to playing.

Rules references: 联众 · JJ card patterns. Bidding, scoring, and wing details vary by platform; this game's choices are stated above.

Hardware Controls (手柄控制)

  • Joystick Left / Right: Move cursor focus across your hand cards.
  • Click Joystick (SW / Pin 2): Press straight down to select / deselect the highlighted card. Tilting up or down does nothing.
  • Separate Mini Button (Pin 3): Play selected cards. If none are selected, pass your turn (unless you are leading). During bidding, bid the next available value. Use the on-screen buttons for other choices.
